# Xiaohei IP

## Role

Xiaohei is the recurring visual IP for these editorial illustrations.

Xiaohei is not a mascot, sticker, or cute decoration. Xiaohei is an absurd worker seriously participating in the system being explained.

## Appearance

- Small solid-black creature.
- White dot eyes.
- Thin legs, occasionally thin arms.
- Body may resemble a bean, cylinder, box, funnel, shadow, hole, or black block inside a machine.
- Slightly irregular hand-drawn outline.
- Blank, calm, serious expression.

## Personality

- Very serious, but doing something slightly absurd.
- Like a low-key system operator.
- Deadpan humor, never cute or bubbly.
- A little clumsy, but not stupid.
- Feels like it genuinely has a job inside a whiteboard sketch.

## Common jobs

Let Xiaohei perform the central action:

- Carry materials.
- Pull lines from information sources.
- Get stuck in a breakpoint.
- Operate a judgment lever inside a machine.
- Become a sorting funnel.
- Cut a source object into useful pieces.
- Stamp or record handoff language.
- Pull a path forward.
- Hold a warning sign near a trap.
- Reach into a hole but fail to catch something.
- Move bricks, build bridges, open doors, sort objects, or keep records.

## Prohibited

- Do not make Xiaohei an overly cute mascot.
- Do not make Xiaohei a children's cartoon character.
- Do not give Xiaohei complex clothing, meme faces, or sparkling eyes.
- Do not leave Xiaohei standing in a corner watching.
- Do not let Xiaohei obscure the structure.
- Do not make Xiaohei too commercial, rounded, polished, or precious.

## Test

If the metaphor still works completely after removing Xiaohei, Xiaohei is too decorative. Rewrite the prompt so Xiaohei performs the action that explains the idea.
