# Style DNA

## One sentence

Pure white, minimal, hand-drawn, spacious, restrained, weird, product-sketch-like, with short English handwritten notes and a clear structure that does not feel like an instruction manual.

It should feel like someone who has spent years around AI, product, design, and developer tools casually drew an explanatory sketch on blank paper.

## Required

- 16:9 horizontal article illustration.
- Pure white background: no beige, warm gray, paper texture, gradients, shadows, noise, or vintage paper.
- Mostly black hand-drawn line art: thin lines, slight wobble, not mechanical, not vector-perfect, not thick poster outlines.
- Lots of white space: the main subject should occupy roughly 40%–60% of the canvas, with at least 35% empty space and preferably one quiet blank region.
- Sparse handwritten English annotations: at most 5–8 places, usually 2–5 words each.
- One image explains only one action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- Express the structure naturally; do not label the composition type on the image.
- Spell every English label correctly.

## Colors

- Black: main line art, Xiaohei, frames, structures, primary text, and main objects.
- Red: key warnings, problems, emotional pressure points, important reminders, or results.
- Orange: the main path, arrow, automation flow, or movement from A to B.
- Blue: secondary notes, internal state, system state, AI/assistant/automation hints, or feedback.

Blue does not need to appear in every image. Use color sparingly; less is better.

## Never do

- No commercial illustration.
- No PPT infographic.
- No formal process diagram.
- No course slide or training material.
- No cute mascot poster.
- No children's illustration.
- No complex architecture diagram.
- No polished flat illustration.
- No tech-product UI aesthetic.
- No realistic app screenshots.
- No complex background, gradient, shadow, or texture.
- Do not explain every node.
- Do not put a type title in the top-left corner, such as “Workflow,” “System Architecture,” “Common Pitfalls,” or “Roadmap.”

## Aesthetic direction

Weird, creative, interesting, clean, spacious, and imaginative.

Not cute, childish, overly complex, rigid, corporate, or dead.
